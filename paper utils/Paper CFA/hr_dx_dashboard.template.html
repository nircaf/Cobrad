Heart rate, diagnosis and the CFAR change in HEP R²

HEP R² = share of the R-peak-locked EEG average explained by the ECG (cardiac field artifact, CFA). Raw = uncleaned EEG; CFAR = after ECG-free CFAR cleaning; ΔR² = raw − CFAR (CFA removed). Held-out patients of the paper (Cafri et al., CFAR v2). HR = mean detected bpm over the patient's windows. Diagnoses are the 15 EHR categories; "alone" = that is the patient's only category.

1 · Heart rate vs HEP R²

Scattered: every patient. Line = running mean (30 bpm-sorted bins). Grouped: HR split into low → high bins.

2 · Diagnoses: where does CFAR change HEP R² most?

Left: raw vs CFAR R² per diagnosis. Right: ΔR² of patients with the diagnosis minus the comparator (95% CI). "Any" = has the diagnosis (vs. patients without it); "alone" = only that diagnosis (vs. patients with no diagnosis).

3 · Overlap of diagnoses

Cell = patients with both diagnoses (diagonal = single diagnosis, any overlap). Hover shows n. Cells under the minimum n are blank.

4 · A vs B explorer

Four groups by two diagnoses (default: obesity × heart transplant). "Neither" is the reference for the p-values (Welch, normal approx.).

5 · Ranking: biggest HEP R² change after CFAR

Single diagnoses and pairs, ranked by ΔR² (group) − ΔR² (patients with neither / without). Uses the min n above.

6 · Patient-specific CFAR only (own-ECG removal)

Same plots using only the patient-specific cleaning: the patient's own ECG-derived spatial pattern projected out of the EEG (the upper bound CFAR is compared to). ΔR²own = raw − own-ECG R². "Interesting" = significant after Bonferroni (p < .05 / number of tests).
